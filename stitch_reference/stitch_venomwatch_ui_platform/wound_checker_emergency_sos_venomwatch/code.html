FIRST-AID STANDARD PROTOCOL

4 Golden-Hour Life-Saving Rules

Standard clinical directives formulated by the World Health Organization (WHO) and National Health Authorities.

01
self_improvement

Stay Calm & Still

Elevated heart rate accelerates venom lymphatic absorption. Lie down flat if feasible and minimize all physical exertion.

PHYSIOLOGICAL TARGET

Maintain resting pulse < 85 BPM

02
airline_seat_legroom_extra

Immobilize Limb

Splint the affected arm or leg with a padded stick or sling. Keep the bitten extremity strictly positioned at or slightly below heart level.

SPLINTING POSITION

Neutral anatomical plane

03
watch_off

Strip Jewelry

Remove rings, bracelets, tight footwear, and restrictive clothing instantly before localized edema and swelling entrap blood flow.

CRITICAL DANGER

Severe acute compartment syndrome

dangerous
04
block

STRICT PROHIBITIONS

DO NOT apply a tourniquet, cut the puncture wounds, apply ice, or use oral suction. Outdated folklore practices cause gangrene and amputation.

LETHAL RISK FACTOR

Accelerates irreversible tissue necrosis

vaccines
Nearby Antivenom Facility Protocol POLYVALENT ASV

Inform paramedics to prepare Polyvalent Antisnake Venom (ASV) upon arrival. Ensure the receiving hospital is equipped with continuous mechanical ventilatory support (BVM/Ventilator) in case of neurotoxic respiratory arrest.

## Brand & Style

The design system embodies tactical biosafety, environmental situational awareness, and venomous wildlife risk telemetry. It addresses emergency field responders, environmental scientists, wildlife management agencies, and public safety teams operating under high-stress conditions. 

The aesthetic is a calibrated fusion of **High-Tech Tactical HUD** and **Refined Environmental Glassmorphism**. The visual field maintains near-pitch black depths reminiscent of deep forest canopies at night, offset by crisp, luminescent optical emeralds and warning signals. The design system prioritizes:
- **Instant Risk Decoupling**: Visual cues immediately separate benign observation from lethally venomous encounters using unmistakable, high-chroma safety states.
- **Mission-Grade Ergonomics**: Ultra-legible typography, high density without cognitive friction, and military-precision metadata layouts.
- **Subdued Luminescence**: Surfaces utilize deep translucent obsidian planes, sub-surface emerald refraction, and crisp border definition to sustain dark adaptation during nocturnal field operations.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast functional color assignments calibrated for low-light legibility and tactical telemetry.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces**: Ground surfaces are anchored in deep charcoal-black (`#0B0F0E` for root canvases and `#111716` for elevated containers and interactive panels). Intermediate layer fills sit at `#16201E` with subtle optical transparency.
- **Primary Signal (Bio-Telemetry Emerald)**: `#10B981` (interactive, active tracks, verified data nodes) with `#059669` as the pressed/deep state. Used sparingly for primary operations and active safety vectors.
- **Secondary Accent (Hydro-Tactical Teal)**: `#0D9488` with highlight steps at `#14B8A6`. Denotes telemetry telemetry channels, sensor nodes, and secondary analytical readouts.
- **Tertiary Anchor (Deep Hunter Forest)**: `#064E3B` serves as muted pill background fills, active state containment strokes, and tactical segment grounding.
- **Critical Status Signals**:
  - **Venomous Danger / Lethal Vector**: `#EF4444` (bright high-visibility alert) and `#DC2626` (solid alert containers, critical hazard rings).
  - **Caution / Advisory State**: `#F59E0B` for non-lethal stings, uncertain species classifications, and cautionary perimeter buffers.
- **Structural Borders & Outlines**: `#1E2E2A` serves as the baseline tactical border, carrying faint emerald luminescence against dark charcoal backdrops.
- **Text & Content Contrast**: Primary typography utilizes crisp off-white (`#F1F5F9` and `#E2E8F0`), while secondary telemetry, metadata labels, and coordinate streams utilize cool tactical slate (`#94A3B8`). Disabled or tertiary references rest at `#475569`.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Space Grotesk** for display headers, data headers, tactical counters, and metadata tags with **Geist** for high-density telemetry, log entries, coordinate maps, and body narrative.

- **Space Grotesk**: Delivers geometric authority and a technical HUD presence. Used for species taxons, hazard alerts, geospatial markers, and numerical readouts. Tactical metadata labels utilize uppercase transformations with expanded letter tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`).
- **Geist**: Provides clean, distraction-free neutral legibility for analytical field notes, treatment protocols, and observation logs. It retains structural clarity even when dense tables and rapid-fire incident logs compress into low-profile viewports.
- **Hierarchy Protocol**: Never pair heavy weights with full-strength colors when displaying passive information. Use `label-sm` in all-caps coupled with `#94A3B8` for telemetry qualifiers (e.g., `LAT/LONG`, `ANTIVENOM AVAILABILITY`, `VENOM POTENCY INDEX`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column tactical fluid system engineered for dense data displays, operational consoles, and rugged handheld field units.

### Spacing Principles
- **Spatial Grid Units**: Built on a strict 4px sub-grid with base standard units of 8px (`space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). 
- **Field Terminal Layouts**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single column with persistent top threat-level banner and pinned bottom emergency antivenom trigger. Margins are set to `margin` (16px), gutters to 16px.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout split between live incident map/sensor feed (5 columns) and triage intelligence stack (3 columns).
  - **Desktop (1025px+)**: Full 12-column HUD layout with a 24px gutter (`gutter-desktop`) and 32px outer canvas margin (`margin-desktop`). Sidebar navigation remains slim, pinning vital tactical metrics and species intelligence logs in asymmetric, high-density panels.
- **Micro-Gaps**: Compact operational clusters (such as coordinate badges, hazard pills, or venom classification matrices) utilize `space-xs` (4px) to retain spatial cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established through **Sub-Surface Glassmorphism**, layered dark charcoal tiers, and emerald-tinted containment borders rather than conventional heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0B0F0E` solid flat background.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: Translucent `#111716` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a crisp 1px border colored `#1E2E2A`. Soft ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Modules / Hover States / Dropdowns)**: Translucent `#16201E` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border color subtly illuminates to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Layer 3 (Critical Alert / Incident Overlays / Modals)**: Translucent `#121817` at 96% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. On danger/venom states, the containment outline transitions to `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.45)` with a faint localized perimeter glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15), 0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft / Precision Technical** corner language (`roundedness: 1`). Rounding is kept restrained to maintain a modern, surgical, military-grade interface rather than a consumer lifestyle appearance.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to status tags, data pills, micro-counters, input elements, checkboxes, and segmented selectors.
- **Container Radius (rounded-lg: 0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to cards, telemetry dashboards, intelligence viewports, and modals.
- **Maximum Soft Radius (rounded-xl: 0.75rem / 12px)**: Restricted strictly to full-bleed alert panels or prominent spatial map container housings.
- **Pill Exception**: Full circular pills are strictly reserved for critical threat level indicators, dynamic ping counters, and live recording badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Tactical Action**: Background `#10B981`, foreground `#0B0F0E` (bold weight for maximum contrast), border none, hover background `#059669`.
- **Secondary / HUD Action**: Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12)`, foreground `#14B8A6`, border 1px solid `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.3)`. Hover: border `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.6)`, background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.24)`.
- **Critical Emergency Trigger (Venom/Dispatch)**: Background `#DC2626`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#EF4444`. Pulsing soft aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Background transparent, foreground `#94A3B8`, border 1px solid `#1E2E2A`. Hover: text `#F1F5F9`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`.

### Hazard Badges & Chips
- **Venomous Status Pill (Danger)**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, text `#EF4444`, border 1px solid `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`. Includes a leading solid red dot marker.
- **Caution / Under Review Pill**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, text `#F59E0B`, border 1px solid `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`.
- **Safe / Non-Venomous Pill**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, border 1px solid `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Telemetry Tag**: Neutral dark surface `#16201E`, text `#94A3B8`, border 1px solid `#1E2E2A`, all-caps `label-sm`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#111716` with a crisp 1px `#1E2E2A` border. Placeholder text in `#475569`. Active text in `#F1F5F9`.
- Focused state: Border transitions to `#10B981`, with a faint inward emerald highlight (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #10B981`).
- Leading icons (such as crosshairs, geolocators, or species search) render in `#94A3B8` and transition to `#10B981` on active input.

### Tactical Cards & Intelligence Containers
- Built on Layer 1 elevation with an internal padding of `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- Incorporates a 1px `#1E2E2A` border. Headers feature an integrated metadata strip separating the title from the live status tag.
- Critical specimen cards feature a dynamic top hairline border (2px) tinted according to hazard classification (`#EF4444` for neurotoxic/cytotoxic dangerous fauna, `#10B981` for verified harmless species).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- Checkboxes: 16x16px square with a 3px radius. Inactive border 1.5px `#1E2E2A`, background `#111716`. Checked state: Background `#10B981`, border `#10B981`, with a dark `#0B0F0E` checkmark glyph.
- Switch Toggles: Track uses `#1E2E2A` with an inset switch thumb of `#94A3B8`. Active switch track glows `#064E3B` with the active thumb illuminating to `#10B981`.

### Data Lists & Specimen Log Tables
- Table rows feature alternating soft-state fills (`transparent` to `rgba(22, 32, 30, 0.4)` on hover).
- Row borders are 1px solid `rgba(30, 46, 42, 0.6)`.
- Specimen listings feature left-aligned taxonomic notation (`headline-sm`), accompanied by right-aligned telemetry badges (e.g., distance, antivenom vial reserves, toxicity index).

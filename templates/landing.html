Community Risk Awareness

Contributing to safer communities through shared location intelligence.

Reported sightings contribute to aggregated geographic risk information, helping communities and authorities understand where snake activity is being reported. This collective data supports better awareness and informed decision-making for public safety.

Your privacy is protected — individual user locations are not exposed publicly.

AI-Assisted Identification

Venom Watch provides AI-generated predictions to support snake-sighting assessment. Predictions may be incorrect and should not replace professional identification or medical advice. Always exercise caution and consult wildlife experts when needed.

For suspected snakebite: Seek emergency medical attention immediately.
